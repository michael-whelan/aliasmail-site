Alias Mail

Privacy policy

The short version

We don't store your emails. We forward them and keep only basic delivery info (sender, subject, time) for 30 days. That's the whole idea of Alias Mail, and it's what the app shows you too.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in address — the email you sign in with and where your aliases forward. You can change it any time in the app.
  • Your aliases — the addresses you create, their label, category and expiry.
  • Delivery metadata — for forwarded mail we keep the sender, subject and time received for 30 days so the app can show you an alias's history. The email content itself is forwarded and not stored.
  • Delivery health — bounce and complaint counts per alias so we can disable an alias that can't be delivered to. These counters are kept with the alias and disappear when it does.

What we never do

  • We never read, store or sell the content of your emails.
  • We never share your addresses with anyone.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or ad trackers in the app or on this site.

Sign-in

Signing in uses a one-time 6-digit code sent to your address. Codes are sent from noreply@auth.aliasmail.app and expire quickly. We keep no passwords.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes your account, all aliases and their history immediately. Addresses are retired and never reused. See how to delete your account, or email support@aliasmail.app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@aliasmail.app.
