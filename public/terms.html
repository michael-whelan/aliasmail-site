Terms of service

1. The service

Alias Mail provides email aliases on aliasmail.app that forward to an inbox you control. The free plan allows 1 live alias with a 30-day lifetime and 30 forwards a month. The Plus plan (€1.99/month or €14.99/year) allows 5 live aliases, custom expiry and 500 forwards a month. Limits are shown in the app.

2. Acceptable use

  • Don't use aliases for spam, phishing, harassment, fraud or anything illegal.
  • Don't use the service to circumvent bans or misrepresent your identity for malicious purposes.
  • Aliases that generate hard bounces or spam complaints are automatically disabled, and accounts that abuse the service can be terminated.

3. Killing and expiry

You can kill an alias at any time. A killed alias stops accepting and forwarding mail immediately and its address is retired — it is never reissued to you or anyone else. Aliases also expire per their set lifetime; expired aliases can be renewed on the same address while your plan allows it.

4. Subscriptions

Plus is billed through Google Play. Purchases, renewals and cancellations are managed with your Google Play account; cancelling stops future charges and the account returns to the Free plan at the end of the billing period. Existing aliases keep running, but expiry is capped to the Free plan's rules.

5. Availability and changes

The service is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind. We may change limits, features or these terms; material changes will be announced in the app or on this site. Continued use after a change means you accept it.

6. Termination and deletion

You can delete your account at any time (see deleting your account), which removes all data immediately. We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms.
