Delete your account

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Settings from the home screen.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, every alias and all delivery history are removed right away. Aliased addresses are retired and never reused — mail sent to them stops arriving.

Without the app

If you can't sign in or no longer have the app, email support@aliasmail.app from the address you signed up with and we'll delete the account for you. We may ask one confirming question so we don't delete the wrong account.

What happens to a Plus subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription by itself. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to stop future charges.
